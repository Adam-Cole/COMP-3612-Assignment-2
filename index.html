About LUGA

LUGA is a tiny cross-continental idea between two friends from opposite sides of the world, Adam Cole (a maple-syrup-loving Canadian with a soft spot for cozy flannels) and Prerana Pradhan (a proud Nepali creative who grew up surrounded by Kathmandu’s colors, textures, and traditions). Together, they dreamed up a brand that blends the heart of Nepal with a touch of Canadian practicality. LUGĀ celebrates the artisans, stories, and style of Nepal while keeping things modern, comfortable, and just a little bit cheeky. Whether you’re here for bold craftsmanship, thoughtful design, or the joy of discovering something new, LUGĀ is your passport to a wardrobe inspired by two cultures, one friendship, and a whole lot of love for great clothing.

This site was built for COMP 3612 – Web Development using semantic HTML5, modern CSS (Flexbox & Grid), vanilla JavaScript, and JSON product data. All the code lives on GitHub: github.com/Adam-Cole/COMP-3612-Assignment-2 .

Poke around, add some pieces to your cart, and imagine the smell of momo and masala chai drifting in from a Kathmandu street.
